01 / Typography III · Research zine

Cold room. Hot signal.

A listening zine about HÖR Berlin, hard techno and finding my sound.

I wanted the zine to carry the energy I feel in a set. Letters push into each other, liquid heat leads the eye and quieter pages give the loud ones more impact. The opening spread shares how THAM helped me discover the sound I connect with.

02 / Typography III · Editorial spreads

Printing is political.

An editorial study of the Detroit Printing Co-op.

I wanted the opening to catch my attention straight away, then give the story room to breathe. I used blue to connect the spreads and kept the longer sections clear and easy to read.

03 / Corporate I · Logo studies

One name. Many forms.

A collection of abstract marks for JABEZ.

I tried different curves, sharp shapes and spaces inside the marks to see how much the same name could change. I kept the colours simple so I could focus on the shapes.

04 / Advertising I · Campaign analysis

A moment in time.

A visual study of the Creative Revolution in advertising.

I looked at three older campaigns that still feel interesting today. I wanted to understand why their ideas worked, then put the ads beside my own thoughts in a clear layout.
