ABOUT / JULIAN MARINO

I LOOK.

DESIGN MEETS SOUND

I find ideas
in what I love.

I’m a graphic design student at George Brown and a DJ. I’m interested in typography, visual identity and the ways that music and street culture shape design.

I love hard techno and listening to full sets. Paying attention to rhythm, mood and transitions also makes me think about how a visual sequence feels.

I like making work with a strong personality. I often start with bold type and colour, then keep adjusting it until the idea feels clear.
